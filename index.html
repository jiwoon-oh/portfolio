About

How I work

I spend most of my effort on the step between a correlation and a decision. A difference in means is easy to produce; establishing that it would survive being acted on is the part that takes the work — matching on the right covariates, holding a unit against itself, correcting for the tests you ran and didn't report.

Three of the four studies here end in a null or a reversal. That wasn't the plan in any of them, and it's the reason I keep them in the portfolio: the useful skill isn't producing an effect, it's knowing when the one you found isn't there.

Each case study documents its own limitations section — what the design cannot identify, and what I would need to close the gap.
